Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-13
  Features:
    - First version. Attack drone launchpad and attack drones, unlocked by the Attack drones research.
    - Drones launch one at a time and circle the launchpad in a V formation until the squad is full (squad size set in the launchpad's panel, 1 to 30).
    - Full squads fly to the nearest charted nest within 1000 tiles, circle it and laser nests, worms and defenders, then move on to nearby nests.
    - 30 second attack run, then the drones crash.
    - Nests retaliate against the launchpad, like artillery.
    - Built on WePlay: Flight Library.
